## English

Socialium is a learning game for social skills: a 3D world where you pause the conversation, choose differently and learn from every choice. Spot pressure and manipulation, hold a warm boundary, and build charisma and confidence.

### What's inside

- 8 arenas: pressure radar, guilt and a warm boundary, fact or story, charisma, mutual attraction, body language, confidence in small steps, and repair after a mistake
- 16 voiced choice moments
- Options lab: dozens of practice scenarios by area
- Learning mode with a hint every turn, and a certification test at the end
- A practice journal saved only on your device
- Hebrew, English, Arabic and Russian

### Installing on Android

1. Open the [Releases page](https://github.com/Scitedra-Labs/Socialium-releases/releases) **on your phone** and download the APK.
2. Open the file. If prompted, allow installs from this source.
3. Tap **Install**.

First Android beta: a test build (debug-signed) with internet permission only.

On iPhone: open [the browser version](https://scitedra-socialium.web.app) in Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen.
